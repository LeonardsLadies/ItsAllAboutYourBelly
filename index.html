Belly Dance Roots

Belly Dancing, or Raqs SharQui, is an ancient art form with roots tracing back thousands of years. Originating from the Middle East and North Africa, it served as a vital part of cultural rituals and celebrations, celebrating the female form through fluid, expressive movements of the torso and hips. While its foundations are deeply historical, the dance has continuously evolved, blending cultural influences and adapting to contemporary sensibilities. Today, it remains a powerful medium for storytelling, physical health, and community, bridging the gap between ancient traditions and modern creative expression.

Evolution of Styles

Belly dance has evolved to encompass a vast array of forms and techniques that honor its diverse history. Some of these styles are showcased below:

European Forms

Turkish Belly Dance: Gypsy (Roman) style features lively, rhythmic movements, while Ottoman style emphasizes theatrical elegance and showmanship.

Oriental Fusion: Blends traditional Middle Eastern dance with Western influences like modern dance, jazz, or ballet.

American Forms

American Tribal Style (ATS): An improvisational, group-focused style that emphasizes cue-based communication between dancers.

American Cabaret: A highly theatrical approach perfect for stage, featuring elaborate costumes, props, and a mix of traditional and contemporary elements.

Folkloric & Props

This includes the study of traditional rhythms and dances like cane (Saidi) dance, the drama of balancing swords, and the mastery of props like Isis Wings, veils, and fan veils that add visual depth to any performance.

Our Belly Dance Styles

Greek Belly Dance

A lively, expressive style often danced to upbeat, traditional Greek rhythms. It embraces a joyful, celebratory spirit with playful finger cymbals (zills) and fluid, dynamic movements.

SharQui Workout

A high-energy belly dance fitness experience! SharQui focuses on continuous movement, breaking down authentic belly dance techniques into fun, easy-to-follow cardio routines.

Belly Dance Fusion

A modern, mesmerizing evolution of the dance. It combines elements of traditional belly dance with strong posture, muscular control, and contemporary influences from various global dance genres.

Essence of Bellydance

A holistic method by Coco Berlin combining traditional dance with Pelvic Floor Integration, mindfulness, and dance medicine. It focuses on spinal mobility, posture, and moving from the inside out.
