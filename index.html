Greek Belly Dance

A lively, expressive style often danced to upbeat, traditional Greek rhythms. It embraces a joyful, celebratory spirit with playful finger cymbals (zills) and fluid, dynamic movements.

SharQui Workout

A high-energy belly dance fitness experience! SharQui focuses on continuous movement, breaking down authentic belly dance techniques into fun, easy-to-follow cardio routines.

Tribal Fusion

A modern, mesmerizing evolution of the dance. It combines elements of traditional belly dance with strong posture, muscular control, and often features earthy, antique-inspired costuming.

Folkloric & Props

Discover the traditional roots of the dance! This includes joyful cane dancing (Saidi), the drama of balancing swords, and understanding the rich cultural rhythms that bring the music to life.

Belly Dance Roots & Evolution

Belly Dancing is an ancient dance form with roots tracing back thousands of years. Also known as Raqs SharQui, it is characterized by its intricate and fluid movements of the torso and hips. It has evolved over the centuries, blending cultural influences and adapting to contemporary styles while maintaining its rich historical significance.

European Forms

Turkish Belly Dance: Gypsy Style (also known as "Roman") incorporates lively, rhythmic movements and vibrant costumes. Ottoman Style features more elaborate, theatrical movements focused on elegance and showmanship.

Oriental Fusion: This style blends traditional Middle Eastern dance with contemporary Western elements like modern dance, jazz, or ballet.

American Forms

American Tribal Style (ATS): Developed in the U.S., ATS emphasizes group improvisation, with dancers following a leader who cues movements in real-time.

American Cabaret: Characterized by its theatrical, entertainment-focused approach, featuring elaborate costumes, props, and a mix of traditional and contemporary dance elements.
